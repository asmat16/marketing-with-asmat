# Calendly — see every strategy call booking

Your site embeds: **https://calendly.com/asmat-llh/15**

## Where bookings appear

1. **Calendly dashboard** — [calendly.com/app/scheduled_events](https://calendly.com/app/scheduled_events)  
   All new bookings show here with name, email, and time.

2. **Email** — Calendly sends notifications to the email on your Calendly account (`asmat.llh@gmail.com`).  
   Enable: Calendly → **Account** → **Notifications**.

3. **Google / Outlook calendar** — Connect in Calendly → **Integrations** → **Calendar** so events block your calendar automatically.

4. **On the website** — Visitors book via:
   - Homepage **#book** section (inline calendar)
   - **/book** page (full-page calendar)

## Confirm it works

1. Open [marketingwithasmat.pro/#book](https://www.marketingwithasmat.pro/#book)
2. Book a test slot (use a personal email)
3. Check **Scheduled Events** in Calendly within 1 minute
